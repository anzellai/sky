## The ADT (shipped at this PR)

```haskell
data Unresolved
    = UnresolvedLit
    | UnresolvedLambdaArg
    | UnresolvedUnknown
    | UnresolvedAmbig
    | UnresolvedCycle
    | UnresolvedSuper
    | UnresolvedError
    | UnresolvedUnbound
    deriving (Eq, Show)
```

Lives in `Sky.Type.Solve` next to `SolvedTypes`. Constructors match the major sentinel categories from the wide grep.

This ADT is the **migration target**. Consumers stay on legacy sentinels for now; per-sentinel migration commits in subsequent PRs convert one writer + its readers in lockstep.

## Why a 1.5-session full migration is risky

The full migration requires either:

1. **Adding `T.TUnresolved Unresolved` as a new `T.Type` constructor** so every pattern match on T.Type gets a new arm. Wide grep for `case ... of T.TVar` + `T.TVar` patterns finds 100+ sites across the codebase — mechanical but touchy.

2. **Keep sentinels as TVars but introduce a typed lookup helper** — e.g. `lookupUnresolved :: T.Type -> Maybe Unresolved`. Doesn't actually defuse the sentinels; just adds an indirection.

3. **Migrate one sentinel at a time** — each PR converts 1 writer + its pattern-matchers. Per the existing `Compile.hs:14125` reader, that's at most 3 sentinels per migration unit.

Option 3 is the right shape but doesn't fit "1.5 sessions for all of them" — it's ~6-8 micro-PRs each taking ~0.25 sessions, totalling 2-3 sessions of mechanical work.

## Carry-forward plan

Per-sentinel migration order (chosen for impact + ease):

1. **`_unresolved` / `_unbound` / `_ambig` (most consequential reader at line 14125)** — one PR migrates all three together since they share a pattern-matcher.
2. **`_unknown` (explicit isWildcardSkyType pattern-matcher)** — one PR.
3. **Writer-only sentinels (`_lit`, `_empty`, `_norm`, `_accessor_placeholder`, `_cycle`, `_super`, `_error`)** — one PR each, no reader migration needed; just convert the writer.
4. **Pattern-side sentinels (`_rec`, `_tup_0`, `_tup_1`, `_list_elem`, `_cons_elem`, `_arg0`)** — one PR for the constraint generator.

Total estimated budget: 2-3 sessions across 4-6 commits.
