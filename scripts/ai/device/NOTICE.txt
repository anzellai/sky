Sky device engine (sky-llama-b11200)

This bundle is the Std.Ai.Device engine of the Sky programming language:
sky_llama.cpp (Sky, MIT licence) compiled over the following third-party
software. Each keeps its own licence.

- llama.cpp and ggml, build b11200 (https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp):
  MIT licence, copyright (c) 2023-2026 The ggml authors. See LICENSE-llama.cpp.
- nlohmann/json, vendored by llama.cpp (https://github.com/nlohmann/json):
  MIT licence, copyright (c) 2013-2026 Niels Lohmann.
- The browser builds link the Emscripten runtime (https://emscripten.org,
  version 6.0.12): MIT licence and University of Illinois/NCSA Open Source
  Licence, copyright (c) 2010-2026 Emscripten authors.
- The WebGPU browser build links emdawnwebgpu from Dawn

  3-Clause licence, copyright 2017-2026 The Dawn and Tint Authors.
