# Sky roadmap

The roadmap to v1 is tracked in public, on GitHub:

- **[Sky v1 roadmap](https://github.com/users/anzellai/projects/2)** (GitHub project 2), the board with the status and the target month of each item.
- The five v1 tracks, one epic each:
  - [#223](https://github.com/anzellai/sky/issues/223) stdlib, language and compiler refinements, bug fixes
  - [#224](https://github.com/anzellai/sky/issues/224) performance and optimisation of the compiler, LSP and tooling, and error messages
  - [#225](https://github.com/anzellai/sky/issues/225) AI-native libraries, a reference AI application, training and refinement in Sky
  - [#226](https://github.com/anzellai/sky/issues/226) pet and production projects on Sky as the bug feedback loop
  - [#227](https://github.com/anzellai/sky/issues/227) lock the stdlib, compiler and tooling, and reach v1 maturity

What has shipped is in [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md). The stability and
deprecation policy is in [VERSIONING.md](VERSIONING.md).

The earlier long-form roadmap document is kept, frozen, at
[docs/history/archive/roadmap-to-v1-2026.md](docs/history/archive/roadmap-to-v1-2026.md).
